Rocky Land Group is a small family partnership. We do not have a fund, a
carry-driven mandate, or a deadline imposed by somebody else's fiscal year.

## The partners

**Will Lansing** — capital, underwriting, and the long view. Thirty years of
looking at dirt and deciding whether it is worth the trouble.

**Art Lansing** — entitlement, construction, and operations. Handles the
permits, the contractors, and everything that goes wrong on a Friday afternoon.

## Where we work

Primarily Montana and eastern Washington, with occasional detours when the
parcel justifies it.
